Frequently asked

  • How does PEAK work? Take or upload a selfie and PEAK gives you an AI facial analysis across 6 areas, plus a personalized glow-up plan.
  • What happens to my photo? Your selfie is analyzed and then deleted immediately. We never store or share it. See our Privacy Policy.
  • How do I manage or cancel PEAK Pro? Open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → PEAK, or Settings inside the App Store. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • How do I restore my subscription? In PEAK, go to Settings → Restore purchases.
  • How do I delete my account and data? In PEAK, go to Settings → Delete account.
